About Presence

MT Presence

MT Presence is a fine art image brand for collectors, founded by the image-maker MT.

We strip away photography's role as a record and build, in a purely visual language, an open space for contemplation. Every work is made for long looking and for keeping, in the hope that each viewer may see, here, into their own heart.

At MT Presence, an image is something to look at again and again. It is in no hurry to explain, nor to be seen and done with; over time, its echo slowly widens.

  • 01

    In the field

    In the hills and by the water, waiting for the light and the animals to come in their own time.

  • 02

    Black and white, and colour

    Black and white to quiet the colour and keep only light and form; colour to hold the feel of a place.

  • 03

    A slower way of seeing

    Slowing down, and giving attention back to what is in front of us: a patch of light, a bird, a ridge.
