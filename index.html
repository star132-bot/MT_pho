Statement

MT CIJIAN

MT CIJIAN is a fine art photography practice founded by image maker MT. The work suspends photography's habitual claim to documentation and moves toward a quieter form of encounter.

Each image is made for long looking and collection. It does not rush to explain itself, and it does not rush to end. It stays in time, allowing a deeper resonance to form.
