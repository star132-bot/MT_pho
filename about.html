About the Practice

MT Presence

A fine art photography practice concerned with weather, surface, distance, and the time required to look.

MT works between abstraction and the visible world. The photographs begin with real places and objects, then stay with the point at which description becomes uncertain: a shoreline held in fog, a plane of stone, or light crossing an ordinary form.

Practice

Images made for long looking

The work treats photography as an encounter rather than a record. Each image is allowed to hold its ambiguity, leaving room for the viewer's own memory and attention.

Bodies of work develop slowly through repeated visits, changing weather, and the relationship between a photograph and the images that surround it. Sequencing is part of the work: no image is asked to stand alone as an explanation.

Availability

Exhibitions, editorial, licensing, and commissions

Selected works and series are available for exhibition, editorial use, licensing discussions, and considered commissions.
